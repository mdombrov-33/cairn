How much do you want to drive?

This sets who handles the busywork — you can loosen or tighten any single control later, in settings, mid-campaign.

NarrativeCompanions act for themselves. Passive discoveries stay quiet. The table still hands you every active check.
Balanced ✓Companions propose combat plans; passive discoveries surface; reaction windows pause with a recommendation.
TacticalYou direct companion combat, equipment, leveling, and checks. Individual controls can still drift from the preset.
What does dying mean here?

Choose the campaign's default stakes. You can change this later in Table settings; the next turn uses the new rule.

PacifistAt 0 HP the danger becomes capture, loss, or another consequence. The campaign does not end in death.
Narrative ✓Death saves hold the moment open; failure returns the character through a lasting story consequence.
HardcoreOne grave. No death saves, no return — the campaign ends where the character does, and the record stays.
