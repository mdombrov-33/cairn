Ilse Marrow PERSON · TOUCHED BY 2 SCENARIOS

Widow of the toll-keeper drowned in the flood of '31. Keeps a private ledger duplicating the toll house's — the extra entry is hers. Secret (narrator-only): she has paid the ferryman's fare eleven times, for eleven crossings no one living took.

River-law, first stone LAW · WORLD-WIDE

"The Weigher counts what crosses. Nothing crosses free." Every ford, ferry, and bridge in the Riverlands operates under it; breaking it is the world's one unforgivable act.
