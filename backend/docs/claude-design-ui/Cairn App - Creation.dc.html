Name
Isolde Ferry
Appearance
River-grey eyes; copper prayer chain; ferry rope burns across both palms.
Backstory
Grew up on the ferry her family has run for six generations. Took orders when the river took her brother. She believes the dead are owed a fare and the living are owed the truth.
Personality
Patient under pressure. Counts costs before promises. Direct with people she trusts.
Goals
Immediate: learn why the bell rings. Life: restore safe passage across the ford.
