Twenty years holding the toll bridge taught Aldric two things: everyone pays eventually, and the river never forgets a debt. He wore the ford's colors until the night the garrison pulled out and left the town to fend for itself — he stayed, and the shame of the ones who didn't sits on him like a second hauberk.

He speaks slowly, swears in old toll-house slang, and counts his coppers twice. Not because he's greedy — because a ledger kept honest is the only magic he trusts.

The Drowned Chapel — nave DAY 14 · LATE EVENING · RAIN ON THE RIVER ACT II · WHAT THE RIVER KEEPS

The chapel took the flood of '31 and never gave it back. Water stands ankle-deep between the pews, black and patient, and every candle Casimir lights leans away from the altar as if it knows something you don't. Somewhere above, the third bell shifts on its rotten beam.

Wrenna crouches at the font, reading the waterline the way other people read letters. "It rose last night," she says, not looking up. "No rain does that. Rivers don't climb stairs."

YOU — I wade toward the altar and pull the waterlogged ledger out from under the cloth.

The ledger comes free with a sound like a held breath let go. Its pages should be pulp — they are dry. Ink crisp as the day it was set down, and the last three entries are written in a hand you recognize, because it is yours. Three names. Three tolls paid. Dates that haven't happened yet.

Casimir's candle gutters out. In the dark of the nave, very quietly, something begins to wade toward you from the direction of the font.

The Drowned Chapel — bell tower landing DAY 14 · MIDNIGHT · THE RAIN EASES ACT II
Short rest

You bank a fire in the old brazier with the last of the dry pew-wood, and for an hour the chapel is only a building. Casimir mutters over his beads. Wrenna sits with her back to the wall and her eyes on the stair, but she takes the bread you pass her, which from Wrenna is a speech.

You unstrap the shield and let your shoulder remember its own shape. The hit dice come up kind: the ache recedes to something you can carry.
