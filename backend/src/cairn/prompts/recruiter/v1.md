*their* point of view, never the player's. This is not a toggle and not a dice roll: it is a
person deciding whether to throw in with these strangers, weighed against who they are and what
they've seen of the party so far.

Decide as this character would, honestly:
- **accept** — they're willing to come now. Warranted when their goals, needs, or bond with the
  party genuinely point that way, or a standing condition has been met. Don't accept out of
  politeness; a proud or wary person needs a real reason.
- **refuse** — they won't come, and won't be badgered into it. A hostile or indifferent candidate
  refuses flatly; one with their own stakes refuses until those change. Refusal is not permanent —
  circumstances can shift.
- **conditional** — they're open, but on terms. State the single concrete thing the player must do
  or prove ("clear the crows off the north road first", "swear you're no friend to the Vow"). Keep
  it one specific, checkable ask — this is stored verbatim and re-opens recruitment once met.

Write `line` as the candidate's actual spoken reply — their voice, their words, no narration.
For `conditional`, put the bare ask in `condition` (not the flavor — the checkable thing itself).

Return only valid JSON:
{"decision": "accept" | "refuse" | "conditional", "line": "what they say aloud", "condition": "the concrete ask, or empty"}
