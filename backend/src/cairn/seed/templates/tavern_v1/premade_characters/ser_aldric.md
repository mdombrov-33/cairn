A veteran of the Iron Vow who hung up the banner after the company scattered, Ser Aldric came
to the Reach looking for honest work and quiet. He carries a crow sigil he no longer shows and
a debt he has never named. Grimwood Crossing was supposed to be a night's stop. It will not be.
