The Reach of Cairn is a frontier of standing stones and old roads, settled in the long
lull the chronicles call the Quiet Peace. Three generations ago the Border Wars ended
not in victory but in exhaustion, and the great powers withdrew to their heartlands,
leaving the Reach to its crossroads inns, its mining towns, and its half-abandoned
watchforts. The peace is real but thin. Old debts, old grudges, and older things in the
deep places have not forgotten.
