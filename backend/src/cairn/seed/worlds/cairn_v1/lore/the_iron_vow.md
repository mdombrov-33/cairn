The Iron Vow was the most disciplined of the free companies that fought the Border Wars —
a mercenary order under a banner of three crows on a black field. When the powers withdrew,
the Vow was left unpaid and scattered. Some hung up their arms and took to trades; others
kept the banner and turned to quieter work — protection, recovery, the settling of debts
that no court would hear. Veterans of the Vow are found across the Reach, recognizable by a
small crow sigil and a refusal to speak of certain campaigns. They keep faith with their own.
