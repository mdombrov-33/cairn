The Quiet Peace is the era the Reach lives in now — three generations since the Border Wars
guttered out. No treaty ended them; the powers simply ran out of coin and bodies and pulled
back to their heartlands. The Reach was left as a buffer nobody governs closely: a country of
crossroads inns, failing mines, and watchforts manned by old men. People here measure time
against the war ("before the Wars", "since the Peace") and carry its debts and grudges quietly.
The Peace is the air everyone breathes — and everyone half-expects it to end.
