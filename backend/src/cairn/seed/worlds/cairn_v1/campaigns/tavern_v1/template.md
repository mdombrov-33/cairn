A crossroads inn on the edge of a haunted forest. A missing merchant, a mysterious stranger,
and a bartender with secrets. The Tavern at Grimwood Crossing is a low-combat opening for new
characters — a place to talk, listen, and pull on the first thread of a larger unraveling in
the Reach of Cairn.
